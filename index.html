FAQ

Quick answers about NoFoodMedWaste.

Is NoFoodMedWaste free?

Yes. Core features are available free of charge.

Does it work for medicines?

Yes. You can track medicine expiry dates and receive reminders.

Does NoFoodMedWaste track cosmetics?

Yes. You can organize cosmetics, track minimum durability dates and manage Period After Opening (PAO) information.

What is PAO?

PAO (Period After Opening) indicates the recommended period of use after a cosmetic product has been opened.

What is Smart Scan?

Smart Scan uses AI to identify products, barcodes and expiry dates.

Is the Android app available?

Currently available through Closed Testing.

Will there be an iPhone version?

iOS support is planned and coming soon.
